# Audit Completion Protocol

This document supplements `AUDIT_REGULATION.md`.

An audit is complete only when it records:

- the exact target and declared role;
- sources and donor materials read;
- material claims and their evidence status;
- rights, licensing, contributors, and third-party status;
- metadata and publication consistency;
- internal or independent review status;
- open risks with category and severity;
- Detox result;
- publication decision: `GO`, `GO WITH EXCLUSIONS`, `HOLD`, or `BLOCK`;
- correction path;
- TraceLog ID and commit or release reference.

The following do not prove successful audit:

- an AI-generated percentage;
- matching expected words in a document;
- simulated output;
- a badge, title, or workflow;
- an internal policy presented as external certification;
- draft code presented as deployed behavior.

When two audits conflict, preserve both, compare their scope and evidence, and record the resolution. Do not average incompatible conclusions.

An internal review must not be described as independent external review.

This supplement is derived from earlier Terra Complete Check, Final Audit, Codex handoff, TraceLog sanitization, FMP completion, and research-pipeline materials. Their useful methods are retained; unsupported scores and certification claims are excluded.
