## Why this file changed

Earlier versions of `terra-legal` presented Terra Public License v1.0 as if it were the active license for the entire Terra ecosystem.

That created material ambiguity because the draft combined:

- copyright and software permissions;
- ethical-use restrictions;
- child-safety requirements;
- privacy expectations;
- commercial fees and revenue sharing;
- certification concepts;
- enforcement language;
- governance and policy principles.

Those functions require separate legal instruments and cannot safely operate as one automatic blanket license across software, scholarship, datasets, archives, brands, and third-party material.

## Current legal structure

The active repository licensing and rights scope is defined by:

- `LICENSE`;
- `LEGAL.md`;
- `LICENSES/CC-BY-4.0.txt`;
- `LICENSES/Apache-2.0.txt`;
- file-level SPDX and REUSE records;
- `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`;
- `TRADEMARK_AND_BRAND_POLICY.md`;
- `RESPONSIBLE_USE_POLICY.md`;
- `CONTRIBUTOR_TERMS.md`.

## Preserved principles

The following principles remain part of Terra governance and responsible-use work:

- Child Safety First;
- protection from exploitation and manipulation;
- privacy and data minimization;
- human oversight;
- documentary and scientific integrity;
- cultural and linguistic respect;
- environmental responsibility;
- partner and commercial accountability.

They are now separated from public copyright and software licensing so that their role and enforceability can be stated honestly.

## Open-source status

The archived Terra Public License draft contains field-of-use and ethical restrictions. It must not be described as an OSI-approved open-source license unless a future version is independently reviewed and satisfies the relevant definition and approval process.

## Future custom-license work

A future Terra responsible-technology license may be developed only for a defined product or controlled licensing use case after:

- professional legal review;
- identification of the actual licensor;
- precise definition of the licensed object;
- contributor and ownership clearance;
- governing-law and dispute-resolution review;
- separation of privacy, conduct, certification, and commercial terms;
- public versioning and migration rules.

Until then, this file is an archive notice, not an active grant of rights.
