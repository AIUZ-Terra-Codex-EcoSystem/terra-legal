## Purpose

This policy preserves the ethical and safety principles previously mixed into the Terra Public License draft.

It governs Terra-managed participation, review, partnership, certification, and deployment processes when it is expressly adopted or incorporated.

It does not silently modify CC BY 4.0, Apache-2.0, MIT, GPL, or another public license.

## Core principle

**Child Safety First**

No Terra-managed project, service, partnership, or certification should expose a child or vulnerable person to avoidable physical, psychological, informational, economic, or developmental harm.

## Responsible-use principles

### Human dignity

- respect autonomy, privacy, cultural identity, and freedom of thought;
- do not treat a person as a data source, behavioral target, or optimization object;
- preserve meaningful human review where decisions may materially affect rights, safety, education, health, livelihood, or access.

### Child and vulnerable-person protection

- collect only data necessary for a declared purpose;
- use age-appropriate explanations and interfaces;
- avoid manipulative, compulsive, exploitative, or deceptive design;
- do not sell or broker child-related behavioral data;
- provide reporting, correction, and deletion paths where applicable;
- use verified consent and authorization processes where required by law.

### Privacy and data minimization

- define purpose before collection;
- minimize collection, retention, and access;
- separate public, restricted, and private traces;
- do not publish secrets, health data, personal identifiers, or confidential records;
- do not infer sensitive traits without a lawful and documented basis.

### Human oversight

- identify accountable human roles;
- provide escalation and appeal paths for material decisions;
- do not present automated output as final legal, medical, scientific, or institutional authority;
- preserve the ability to stop, correct, or withdraw a system.

### Anti-manipulation

Do not use Terra-managed systems for:

- covert behavioral steering;
- dark patterns;
- artificial urgency or scarcity;
- deceptive authority simulation;
- targeted exploitation of fear, dependency, poverty, trauma, illness, or age;
- hidden political, commercial, or ideological profiling.

### Non-discrimination

- review foreseeable disparate effects;
- avoid unjustified exclusion based on protected or sensitive characteristics;
- support multilingual and culturally understandable access where the project claims such scope;
- distinguish evidence from stereotypes or unsupported inference.

### Scientific and documentary integrity

- distinguish evidence, interpretation, hypothesis, vision, and implementation status;
- do not fabricate citations, validation, legal authority, users, partners, metrics, or deployments;
- state uncertainty and limitations;
- preserve source provenance and change history.

### Security

- use reasonable access controls and secret management;
- report vulnerabilities through the declared security channel;
- do not intentionally weaken safeguards or create concealed access paths;
- prioritize protective action where children or vulnerable persons may be affected.

### Cultural and linguistic respect

- avoid claiming universal cultural legitimacy without evidence;
- review names, symbols, translations, and public claims in the relevant languages and contexts;
- do not appropriate protected, sacred, or community-specific symbols in a misleading way.

### Environmental responsibility

Where material, infrastructure, or computational impact is relevant:

- identify significant resource use;
- avoid unsupported environmental claims;
- prefer proportionate and repairable systems;
- disclose material trade-offs rather than using sustainability as branding alone.

## Prohibited conduct within Terra-managed surfaces

Terra-managed services, repositories, partnerships, or certification programs may refuse, suspend, or terminate participation involving:

- sexual exploitation or abuse of children;
- trafficking, grooming, or predatory targeting;
- non-consensual surveillance;
- sale of sensitive child data;
- intentionally addictive design directed at children;
- discriminatory denial of essential access;
- deliberate falsification of scientific, legal, safety, or provenance records;
- malware, credential theft, hidden backdoors, or unauthorized access;
- impersonation of Terra, FMP, the Fractal Metascience Foundation, or an official partner;
- misuse of certification or endorsement marks.

This list governs Terra-managed relationships. It does not amend the permissions of an independent public license unless incorporated into a separate enforceable agreement.

## Review and response

A responsible-use concern should be handled through:

1. intake and preservation of necessary evidence;
2. immediate safety action where required;
3. conflict-of-interest review;
4. proportionate investigation;
5. documented decision and reasons;
6. correction, appeal, or restoration path where appropriate;
7. protected reporting and data minimization.
