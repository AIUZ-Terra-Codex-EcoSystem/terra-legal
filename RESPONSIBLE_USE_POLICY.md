# Terra Responsible Use Policy

**Repository:** `terra-legal`  
**Status:** governance and safety policy  
**Relationship to licenses:** policy layer, not a hidden restriction on standard public licenses

## Purpose

This policy preserves the ethical and child-safety substance previously mixed into custom license drafts, while keeping it separate from copyright and software permissions.

It applies directly to Terra-operated services, official communities, managed deployments, partnerships, certification programs, and contracts that expressly incorporate it.

For independently redistributed materials under CC BY 4.0, Apache-2.0, MIT, GPL, or another standard public license, this policy should be treated as an ethical and governance expectation unless separately made binding through an applicable agreement, terms of service, or participation rule.

## Core principle

**Child Safety First**

No Terra-labelled deployment should knowingly create, intensify, or conceal harm to children, vulnerable persons, learners, families, communities, or the living environment.

## Responsible-use duties

### 1. Child safety

Terra-controlled or Terra-certified child-facing systems must:

- minimize data collection;
- avoid manipulative or addictive mechanics;
- avoid targeted advertising to children;
- provide meaningful adult or guardian controls where legally required;
- provide understandable safety notices;
- maintain incident reporting and response channels;
- avoid hidden profiling or exploitative behavioral optimization.

### 2. Privacy and data minimization

Systems should collect only necessary data, state the purpose of processing, protect sensitive data, and define retention and deletion paths.

This policy does not authorize processing personal data merely because a repository is public or open licensed.

### 3. Human oversight

AI-assisted systems should preserve meaningful human accountability in educational, safety, medical, legal, financial, or high-impact contexts.

### 4. Anti-manipulation

Do not use Terra-linked systems for:

- deceptive persuasion;
- dark patterns;
- hidden surveillance;
- exploitative emotional profiling;
- coercive scoring;
- manipulation of minors or vulnerable persons.

### 5. Cultural and linguistic respect

Localization should respect language, culture, religion, family structures, and community context without claiming unsupported universal authority.

### 6. Environmental and social responsibility

Where Terra work concerns ecology, infrastructure, extraction, logistics, or urban systems, it should avoid misleading sustainability claims and should identify evidence, limits, and affected communities.

### 7. Incident reporting

Official Terra projects should provide a reporting channel for:

- child-safety concerns;
- privacy or security incidents;
- misuse of marks;
- deceptive certification claims;
- harmful deployment;
- serious provenance or licensing violations.

## Prohibited official use

Terra-operated, Terra-certified, or Terra-partner systems must not be used to:

- sell children's personal data;
- bypass parental or guardian protections where legally required;
- target children with manipulative ads;
- create hidden backdoors to sensitive data;
- impersonate Terra certification or endorsement;
- misrepresent a draft theory, draft license, or prototype as validated public infrastructure;
- conceal third-party rights or authorship.

## Enforcement inside Terra-controlled surfaces

Depending on severity, Terra maintainers may:

- request clarification or correction;
- suspend certification or partnership claims;
- remove a project from official listings;
- revoke permission to use marks;
- issue public correction notices;
- require remediation before further collaboration;
- refer child-safety or security issues to appropriate authorities where legally required.

## Relationship to the Terra Universal Convention

This policy operationalizes parts of the Convention for repository and deployment governance. The Convention remains an ethical and institutional framework, while this policy defines practical responsible-use boundaries.

## Relationship to licensing

This policy does not replace `LICENSE.md` and does not secretly modify CC BY 4.0 or Apache-2.0. Binding legal duties must be introduced through the proper legal instrument for the relevant context.

## Review status

This policy is ready for governance use and should still receive professional legal review before being incorporated into commercial, hosted-service, certification, or child-facing deployment contracts.
