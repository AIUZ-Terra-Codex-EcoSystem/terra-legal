# Third-Party Notices and Rights Register

**Repository:** `terra-legal`  
**Status:** living register; absence from this file is not proof that no third-party rights exist

## Purpose

This register distinguishes repository-authored material from external licenses, standards, quotations, templates, software components, images, and other third-party material.

A repository-wide license does not override third-party rights.

## Current recognized third-party legal texts and standards

### Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International

- SPDX identifier: `CC-BY-4.0`
- Canonical source: Creative Commons
- Repository copy: `LICENSES/CC-BY-4.0.txt`
- Status: official standard license text; not authored by the Terra project

### Apache License 2.0

- SPDX identifier: `Apache-2.0`
- Canonical source: Apache Software Foundation
- Repository copy: `LICENSES/Apache-2.0.txt`
- Status: official standard license text; not authored by the Terra project

### SPDX License List and identifiers

- Source: SPDX / Linux Foundation collaborative project
- Use in this repository: standardized license identifiers and expressions
- Status: external standard and reference system

### REUSE Specification

- Source: Free Software Foundation Europe / REUSE project
- Use in this repository: file-level copyright and licensing structure
- Status: external specification and implementation guidance

### Keep a Changelog and Semantic Versioning references

- Use: documentary format references in `CHANGELOG.md`
- Rights: remain with their respective authors and projects

## External legal and ethical references

Repository documents may refer to legal or policy instruments such as:

- United Nations Convention on the Rights of the Child;
- GDPR;
- COPPA;
- UNESCO Recommendation on the Ethics of Artificial Intelligence;
- other national or international legal instruments.

Reference, quotation, or discussion does not imply authorship, ownership, endorsement, official interpretation, or incorporation of the full external instrument into the repository license.

## Donor and source materials

Files derived from or bridged to other Terra, FMP, or AIUZ repositories must identify:

- donor repository;
- source path or version;
- whether text is copied, condensed, translated, or restructured;
- applicable source license;
- material changes.

## Images, logos, and media

Unless a file-level notice states otherwise:

- third-party logos remain the property of their respective owners;
- screenshots may include protected interface or brand elements;
- photographs, scans, maps, diagrams, and artwork require their own rights record;
- public availability is not equivalent to public-domain status.

## Software dependencies and GitHub Actions

Workflow references, actions, scripts, and external libraries retain their own licenses and notices.

Before release, the repository should record:

- component or action name;
- source repository;
- version or commit pin;
- license;
- required notice;
- security and maintenance status.

## Exclusion rule

The default license for an original Terra file does not apply to embedded or adjacent third-party material where the project lacks authority to license it.

Where practical, third-party material should be:

- excluded from the repository;
- linked rather than copied;
- stored in a clearly marked directory;
- accompanied by a file-level rights notice;
- documented in this register.
