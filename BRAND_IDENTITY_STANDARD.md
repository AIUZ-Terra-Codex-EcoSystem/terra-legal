# Terra Brand Identity Standard

**Repository:** `terra-legal`  
**Role:** normative design and identity bridge  
**Creator:** Abdurashid Abdukarimov — ORCID 0009-0000-6394-4912

## Purpose

This file translates Terra Brand Book v2.0 into a restrained donor standard for legal, governance, research, repository, and publication-facing surfaces.

The goal is recognition, accessibility, and continuity. It is not promotional decoration.

## Identity principles

1. Human-readable first.
2. Child safety and dignity.
3. Cultural respect.
4. Accessibility, including readable contrast, keyboard use, and screen-reader compatibility.
5. Traceability for material public-identity changes.
6. Role clarity across legal, theory, archive, publication, and practice layers.
7. Detox against exaggerated or unsupported visual claims.

## Core palette

- Terra Green: `#2E8B57` — ecosystem and continuity.
- Terra Blue: `#4A90E2` — knowledge and technology.
- Terra Orange: `#FF8C42` — process and creative work.
- Terra Purple: `#7B66DC` — theory and reflective layers.
- Deep Gray: `#2C3E50` — primary text.
- Soft Gray: `#F5F5F5` — neutral background.
- White: `#FFFFFF` — clean reading surface.
- Light Blue: `#E8F4FD` — secondary information surface.

Status colors:

- Success: `#27AE60`;
- Warning: `#F39C12`;
- Error: `#E74C3C`;
- Information: `#17A2B8`.

## Typography

Preferred families:

- headings: Montserrat;
- body: Inter;
- code: Fira Code.

Fallbacks must remain open and readable. A document must not fail when these fonts are unavailable.

## Layout

- base spacing unit: 8 px;
- common spacing: 16, 24, and 32 px;
- maximum public-page width: approximately 1200 px;
- narrower reading width for long legal text;
- minimum side padding: 20 px on mobile and 40 px on desktop;
- cards may use 12 px radius, subtle shadow, and a 1 px border when they improve navigation.

## Legal and normative surfaces

Legal and governance pages use a calm identity:

- light neutral background;
- dark readable text;
- Terra Green as primary accent;
- Terra Blue for links and information;
- Terra Orange for process or warning emphasis;
- Terra Purple for theory or reflective bridge sections.

Avoid animated backgrounds, flashing elements, excessive motion, and promotional effects on legal pages.

## Symbols

Stable documentary symbols may include:

- globe — ecosystem;
- shield — safety and governance;
- seedling — learning and growth;
- DNA or weave — continuity and living structure;
- trace line — provenance;
- document — public knowledge.

Emoji are optional navigation aids, not legal marks or proof of status.

## Diagram rules

For fractal or systems diagrams:

- central node: Terra Purple;
- primary branches: Terra Blue;
- secondary nodes: Terra Green;
- process connections: Terra Orange.

Every diagram also requires text labels. Color alone must not carry meaning.

## Review sequence

1. identify the surface role;
2. apply the visual tokens;
3. check contrast and keyboard access;
4. check cultural and child-safety risks;
5. check naming consistency;
6. run Detox;
7. record material identity changes in TraceLog.
