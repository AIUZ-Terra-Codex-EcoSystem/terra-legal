## Typography

- headings: Montserrat, with Arial and sans-serif fallbacks;
- body: Inter, with Arial and sans-serif fallbacks;
- code: Fira Code, with Consolas and monospace fallbacks;
- long legal reading: Georgia, with Times New Roman and serif fallbacks.

## Spacing

- base unit: 8 px;
- standard intervals: 16, 24, 32, and 48 px;
- card radius: 12 px;
- small radius: 6 px;
- standard border: 1 px neutral line;
- standard shadow: subtle and non-essential.

## Terra Legal mapping

- page: Soft Gray;
- cards: White;
- primary text: Deep Gray;
- primary accent: Terra Green;
- links: accessible darkened Terra Blue;
- theory bridge: Terra Purple;
- process marker: accessible darkened Terra Orange.

## Accessibility

- use readable text contrast;
- do not rely on color alone;
- provide visible keyboard focus;
- respect reduced-motion preferences;
- label icons with text;
- keep legal text usable without custom fonts or JavaScript.

## Diagram mapping

- central node: Terra Purple;
- primary branches: Terra Blue;
- secondary nodes: Terra Green;
- process links: Terra Orange;
- neutral context: Deep Gray.

## Boundary

These variables provide visual continuity. They do not establish legal status, ownership, certification, or technical capability.
