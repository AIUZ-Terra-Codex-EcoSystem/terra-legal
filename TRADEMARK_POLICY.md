# Terra Trademark and Brand Policy

**Repository:** `terra-legal`  
**Status:** brand and naming governance policy  
**Relationship to licenses:** trademark and identity layer, separate from copyright and software licenses

## Purpose

This policy protects Terra ecosystem names, visual identity, badges, and certification language from confusion, false endorsement, and misuse.

Open content and software licenses in this repository do not grant trademark rights.

## Protected names and marks

This policy applies to official or potentially official use of:

- `Terra` in Terra ecosystem context;
- `AIUZ`;
- `Fractal Metascience Foundation`;
- `FMP` when used as a source-identifying framework name;
- `NULLO`, `PLT`, `UCOMM`, `EUO`, and related framework identifiers;
- `Invisible Shadow` when used as an official Terra/FMP ontology or project identifier;
- official logos, badges, emblems, visual marks, and certification marks;
- `Terra Certified`, `Terra Legal`, and similar official status or endorsement phrases.

This list may be expanded by Name Cards and the Naming Registry.

## Allowed use without separate permission

You may generally use the names to:

- truthfully refer to the project;
- cite repository materials;
- link to official repositories;
- identify compatibility or dependency in a non-confusing way;
- make commentary, criticism, teaching, or research references;
- preserve attribution required by a content or software license.

Such use must not imply endorsement, certification, partnership, or official status unless that status has been granted.

## Use requiring permission

Permission is required to:

- use official logos or badges in a product or service name;
- claim certification, endorsement, partnership, or official approval;
- create confusingly similar project branding;
- register domains, accounts, apps, or companies that imply official Terra control;
- use Terra names in commercial product names;
- modify official marks and present them as official;
- use identity elements in a way that conflicts with child-safety or responsible-use rules.

## Prohibited use

Do not use Terra names or marks to:

- mislead users about source, control, affiliation, or certification;
- imply that a fork is the official repository;
- present a draft license, draft theory, or prototype as validated official infrastructure;
- sell unauthorized certification or compliance status;
- obscure authorship or third-party rights;
- promote child-safety, privacy, or AI claims that have not been validated.

## Forks and adaptations

Forks and adaptations may identify the original source, but should use clear language such as:

- `fork of terra-legal`;
- `based on Terra materials`;
- `unofficial adaptation`;
- `not endorsed by Terra maintainers`.

Avoid names that make a fork look like the official source.

## Certification language

Certification, compliance, audit, validation, or partner status may be used only when:

- a written approval exists;
- the scope is stated;
- the date and version are stated;
- the relevant audit or validation record exists;
- the status has not expired or been revoked.

## Relationship to Brand Identity Standard

Visual appearance should follow:

- `BRAND_IDENTITY_STANDARD.md`;
- `VISUAL_IDENTITY_GUIDE.md`;
- `NAMING_STANDARD.md`;
- `naming/registry/`.

Brand identity supports recognition. It does not create legal certification by itself.

## Enforcement

Possible responses to misuse include:

- clarification request;
- takedown request;
- removal from official listings;
- revocation of permission;
- public correction;
- contractual enforcement where applicable;
- legal action where necessary.

## Registration status

This policy does not claim that every listed name or mark is a registered trademark in every jurisdiction. Registration, ownership, and enforcement require separate professional review and jurisdiction-specific action.
