# Terra Publication Rule

This file defines the publication boundary and publication behavior for the Terra legal donor repository.

## What belongs here

- stable legal texts;
- governance documents;
- participation rules;
- security and reporting guidance;
- bridge documents that other repositories can inherit;
- extracted documentary protocol surfaces where exact donor relation is stated.

## What does not belong here

- raw archive dumps;
- duplicate theory corpora;
- temporary exports;
- auto-generated noise;
- private operational secrets;
- false "complete package" claims for materials that actually belong to theory, corpus, or archive layers.

## Publication principles

Public material should be:

1. intentional;
2. readable;
3. reviewable by humans;
4. safe for open publication;
5. linked to a clear repository role;
6. traceable to donor sources where extraction occurred;
7. packaged in a way that preserves documentary meaning rather than flattening it.

## Living publication rule

Publication surfaces in Terra are living.
They may stabilize temporarily for release or archival deposit, but they must not pretend that one snapshot exhausts the whole system.

## Cross-repository rule

If a file is primarily theoretical, move or mirror it into a theory repository.
If it is primarily a corpus or evidence object, keep it in a corpus repository.
If it is primarily a documentary donor rule, bridge, or normative surface, `terra-legal` may hold it.
If it is only shadow or backup material, keep it outside the public legal layer.

## Packaging rule

Publication inside `terra-legal` should follow:

- `FULL_PACKAGING_PROTOCOL.md`
- `RULE_0_SOURCE_FIRST_PROTOCOL.md`
- `DETOX_ENGINE_PROTOCOL.md`

No public package should be assembled here without role clarity, provenance, and donor mapping.
