# Governance of terra-legal

## Repository role

`terra-legal` is the normative donor repository for Terra legal, governance, validation, audit, publication, naming, identity, TraceLog, responsible-use, and licensing-strategy documents.

It is not a legislature, court, law firm, state authority, or internationally ratified body.

## Decision classes

### Editorial decisions

Examples:

- typographical corrections;
- broken-link repair;
- formatting;
- non-substantive metadata correction;
- accessibility improvement that does not alter meaning.

Requirements:

- source and affected files identified;
- meaning preserved;
- validation completed;
- normal maintainer review.

### Normative decisions

Examples:

- changes to governance, validation, audit, publication, naming, identity, TraceLog, or responsible-use rules;
- new donor protocols;
- material changes in repository role or inheritance.

Requirements:

- dedicated branch or pull request;
- source-first record;
- rationale and alternatives;
- Detox before, during, and after;
- TraceLog entry;
- maintainer approval.

### Protected legal decisions

Examples:

- license selection or relicensing;
- copyright ownership;
- contributor terms;
- trademark or brand permissions;
- privacy and child-data obligations;
- commercial terms;
- governing law, jurisdiction, dispute resolution, warranties, or liability;
- certification or partnership status.

Requirements:

- dedicated proposal or issue;
- repository and file-level rights audit;
- contributor, dependency, third-party, publisher, or partner review as applicable;
- exact change set in a reviewable pull request;
- TraceLog record;
- explicit Lead Maintainer decision;
- professional legal review where the decision may create material obligations or risk.

## Review process

1. **Proposal** — state the problem, sources, affected files, target outcome, and risks.
2. **Classification** — editorial, normative, protected legal, security, or emergency safety.
3. **Evidence review** — inspect donor texts, repository history, current law or official standards where relevant, and third-party rights.
4. **Draft** — prepare the smallest coherent change on a branch.
5. **Validation** — verify links, metadata, licensing scope, provenance, accessibility, and internal consistency.
6. **Decision** — Lead Maintainer approves, rejects, requests revision, or blocks pending external review.
7. **Merge and release** — merge only after required conditions are met; release separately where necessary.
8. **Post-change audit** — re-read the public surface and record unresolved risks.

No change becomes valid merely because an AI generated it, a file was committed, or a badge appears in a README.

## Consultation

Public or specialist consultation may be used when it adds relevant evidence.

Consultation is not represented as a formal vote unless:

- eligible participants are defined;
- the proposal and voting rules are published in advance;
- conflicts of interest are disclosed;
- the result is recorded;
- the Lead Maintainer states the legal effect of the result.

The repository currently has no standing parliament, membership assembly, or automatic supermajority mechanism.

## Child-safety and security emergency

Where a credible risk concerns child exploitation, exposed personal data, active credential compromise, malware, or immediate physical or informational harm, the maintainer may take temporary protective action before ordinary review.

Emergency action should:

- minimize disclosure;
- preserve necessary evidence;
- remove or restrict harmful material;
- avoid repeating sensitive data in public logs;
- receive a post-action review and TraceLog record.

Emergency handling does not justify permanent hidden governance.

## Conflicts of interest

A reviewer should disclose a material conflict involving:

- financial interest;
- authorship or ownership dispute;
- partner or employer relationship;
- pending litigation or enforcement;
- personal involvement in the reported conduct.

Where practical, conflicted review should be separated from factual evidence collection and an independent specialist should be consulted.

## Transparency

Material governance records should identify:

- decision owner;
- sources and evidence;
- affected files;
- rationale;
- alternatives considered;
- unresolved risks;
- review status;
- commit, pull request, release, or issue reference.

Sensitive evidence must not be published merely to demonstrate transparency.

## Relationship to licenses and policies

- `LEGAL.md` defines repository licensing scope.
- `RELICENSING_PROTOCOL.md` governs license migration.
- `CONTRIBUTOR_TERMS.md` governs contribution certification.
- `RESPONSIBLE_USE_POLICY.md` governs Terra-managed ethical expectations.
- `TRADEMARK_AND_BRAND_POLICY.md` governs identity and endorsement claims.
- `TERRA_UNIVERSAL_CONVENTION.md` remains an internal ethical and governance charter, not a substitute for law or license.
