## Source-first standard

`terra-legal` must not fabricate normative structures in isolation.
When a rule, directive, protocol, name, identity element, or continuity bridge is added here, it must be traceable to an existing donor text, operator instruction, or extracted protocol surface.

## Local obligations

- keep the public surface readable for human review first;
- keep provenance visible;
- preserve separation between donor text, bridge text, and repository-local instructions;
- do not flatten theory, archive, governance, audit, identity, and tooling into one undifferentiated file;
- do not introduce fake DOI, fake authority, unverifiable claims, or unreviewed public names;
- prefer living continuity over rigid version fetishism;
- keep child safety, detoxification, explicit operator control, and anti-surveillance safeguards intact;
- record material changes in TraceLog;
- apply naming and visual-identity checks before public release.

## Packaging rule

Normative packaging inside `terra-legal` follows the Terra living approach:

- literal donor texts where exact wording matters;
- bridge files where cross-repository coherence matters;
- explicit provenance notes where extraction or condensation was necessary;
- public TraceLog records for material documentary changes;
- identity files that improve legibility without importing promotional residue.
