## Purpose

These terms clarify what a contributor represents when submitting code, documentation, translation, legal commentary, design material, or another contribution to this repository.

They do not transfer ownership automatically and do not authorize the contributor to submit material they do not have the right to contribute.

## Contributor certification

By intentionally submitting a contribution for inclusion, the contributor certifies that:

1. the contribution is original to the contributor, or the contributor has sufficient permission to submit it;
2. any third-party material is identified with its source and license;
3. the contribution does not knowingly include confidential information, personal data, credentials, or unlawfully obtained material;
4. the contributor has disclosed any employer, client, publisher, grant, co-author, or contractual restriction that may affect the contribution;
5. the contributor agrees that the contribution may be distributed under the applicable license identified for the target file or directory;
6. the contributor has not relied on an AI system or another tool to create false citations, false authorship, false provenance, or material copied without permission;
7. the contributor accepts the Code of Conduct and repository review process.

## Applicable license

A contribution follows the license of the file or directory into which it is accepted, unless the pull request and maintainer decision explicitly state another compatible license.

Typical target licenses are:

- `CC-BY-4.0` for original documentary material;
- `Apache-2.0` for original software and implementation material.

A contribution to an unclassified file does not become licensed merely because it was proposed. Classification is required before merge.

## Ownership

Contributors retain copyright in their original contributions unless a separate written assignment states otherwise.

By submitting an accepted contribution, the contributor grants the rights required by the applicable repository license.

No assignment to the Fractal Metascience Foundation, Terra, FMP, AIUZ, a GitHub organization, or a maintainer is implied without a separate written agreement.

## Attribution

Contributor attribution should be preserved in:

- Git history;
- pull requests;
- source headers or REUSE metadata where applicable;
- scholarly contributor statements where the contribution qualifies for authorship or another recognized role.

Minor editorial, formatting, or administrative work does not automatically create scholarly co-authorship.

## Artificial intelligence assistance

AI-assisted contributions are permitted only when a human contributor:

- reviews the result;
- verifies sources and rights;
- accepts responsibility for the submission;
- identifies material AI assistance when relevant to provenance or publication rules;
- removes confidential, fabricated, or copied material.

An AI system is not treated as a legal author, copyright owner, maintainer, or accountable contributor.

## Legal and policy contributions

Changes to licenses, contributor terms, governance, privacy, child-safety rules, commercial terms, or trademark policy require:

- a dedicated issue or proposal;
- source-first review;
- explicit identification of jurisdictional assumptions;
- TraceLog entry;
- maintainer approval;
- professional legal review where the change may create material legal obligations.

## Sign-off

For material contributions, the pull request should include:

`Signed-off-by: Full Name <email>`

The sign-off confirms the contributor certification stated above. It is not a copyright assignment.

## Rejection or removal

A contribution may be rejected, reverted, or removed when:

- rights are unclear;
- required attribution or notices are missing;
- confidential or personal material is exposed;
- the target license is incompatible;
- provenance is fabricated or incomplete;
- the contribution creates a safety, security, or legal risk;
- the contribution does not fit the repository role.

Removal should preserve a minimal TraceLog record without repeating protected material.

## No legal representation

Acceptance of a contribution does not create employment, partnership, agency, legal representation, or entitlement to compensation unless a separate written agreement states otherwise.
