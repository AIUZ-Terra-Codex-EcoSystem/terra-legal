## Purpose

This policy separates permission to reuse content or software from permission to use Terra-related names, logos, badges, certification language, and visual identity.

A copyright or software license does not automatically grant trademark rights or permission to imply endorsement.

## Covered identifiers

This policy applies, to the extent rights exist, to identifiers including:

- Terra;
- AIUZ Terra Codex;
- Fractal Metascience Foundation;
- Fractal Metascience Paradigm and FMP as source identifiers;
- Invisible Shadow as a named Terra/FMP framework component;
- Terra Legal;
- Terra certification, audit, validation, quality, partnership, and status badges;
- official logos, emblems, wordmarks, visual marks, and distinctive identity combinations.

This list does not claim that every identifier is registered in every jurisdiction.

## Uses generally permitted without prior written permission

Subject to accuracy, good faith, and applicable law, the following uses are generally permitted:

- truthful reference to a Terra or FMP project;
- citation and attribution;
- linking to an official repository or publication;
- factual comparison, review, research, criticism, or commentary;
- identifying compatibility without implying endorsement;
- unmodified screenshots for teaching, reporting, or documentation;
- stating that material was derived from a specifically identified repository, where the underlying license permits the reuse.

## Conditions for nominative use

A permitted reference should:

- use only what is necessary to identify the project;
- avoid suggesting sponsorship, certification, partnership, or approval;
- distinguish the user's project from the official Terra project;
- preserve attribution and source links where reasonably practicable;
- identify modifications where modified content is shown.

## Uses requiring prior written permission

Prior written permission is required for:

- using an official Terra or FMP mark as the primary name or logo of a product, service, company, event, token, certification, or repository;
- modifying an official logo or certification badge;
- presenting a fork or derivative as the official project;
- claiming partnership, accreditation, certification, authorization, or endorsement;
- using Terra marks in paid advertising in a way likely to imply affiliation;
- registering confusingly similar domains, accounts, organizations, company names, or marks;
- selling merchandise primarily branded with official Terra marks;
- issuing a badge or certificate that could be mistaken for an official Terra credential.

## Prohibited uses

Do not:

- impersonate the maintainer, Foundation, ecosystem, or an official repository;
- use a mark in connection with fraud, exploitation, deceptive fundraising, malware, surveillance, or unlawful activity;
- create false partnership, award, certification, compliance, or institutional-status claims;
- use child-safety language as a misleading commercial guarantee;
- present an unreviewed AI-generated mark as an official registered mark;
- use cultural or sacred symbols as Terra marks without appropriate source, context, and permission review.

## Forks and derivatives

A fork may state:

> Forked from `AIUZ-Terra-Codex-EcoSystem/terra-legal`.

A fork should not use the same primary name, logo, or official badge in a way that creates confusion.

A clear alternative name and a visible statement of independence are required for public derivative projects.

## Certification and status claims

Terms such as:

- Terra Certified;
- Terra Approved;
- Terra Platinum;
- Official Terra Partner;
- FMP Validated;
- Legal Compliance Verified;

must not be used unless a documented certification or partnership process exists and the specific claim has been issued through that process.

Repository presence, a copied badge, a pull request, or a link to `terra-legal` does not itself create certification or official ecosystem status.

## Visual identity

Visual use should follow:

- `BRAND_IDENTITY_STANDARD.md`;
- `VISUAL_IDENTITY_GUIDE.md`;
- the naming registry;
- accessibility and cultural-review rules.

The palette and design tokens may be reused only to the extent their file-level license permits. Such reuse does not grant an endorsement claim.

## Domain and account names

Before using a Terra-related name for a public domain, organization, package, application, or social account:

1. check the Naming Registry;
2. document search ambiguity and collision risk;
3. obtain operator approval where the name implies official status;
4. complete legal or trademark review where commercial or institutional use is planned.

## Enforcement approach

Where confusion or misuse occurs, the preferred sequence is:

1. factual clarification;
2. request for correction;
3. reasonable cure period where safety permits;
4. platform or contractual enforcement;
5. legal action where necessary and supported by applicable rights.

Urgent fraud, child-safety, credential theft, or impersonation risks may require immediate action.
