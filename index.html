Normative donor layer for file-scoped licensing, governance, responsible use, validation, audit, publication, naming, identity, TraceLog, and continuity.

This repository is not the whole Terra archive or theory corpus, and it is not a law firm, court, treaty body, or automatic licensing mechanism for linked repositories.

Stabilization status

The former appearance that CC BY 4.0, CC0-1.0, and Terra Public License v1.0 simultaneously governed the whole repository has been replaced by an explicit file-scoped structure.

  • No single blanket license applies to every file.
  • The former Terra Public License is preserved as an archived draft.
  • Historical grants are not represented as revoked.
  • Unclassified historical files remain pending rights review.

Operating sequence

  1. Read the relevant sources and current rights state under Rule 0.
  2. Classify the object: documentation, software, data, media, brand, third-party material, or archive.
  3. Apply Detox before, during, and after the change.
  4. Work through a reviewable branch or pull request for material changes.
  5. Preserve historical grants, provenance, aliases, and exclusions.
  6. Validate licensing scope, metadata, links, and public claims.
  7. Record material decisions in TraceLog.
